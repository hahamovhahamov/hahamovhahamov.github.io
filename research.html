Research

Dissertation · PhD expected Oct. 2026 The Space Age: Technological Prophecy and the Political Economy of Big Tech

Committee: Matthew Lassiter, Perrin Selcer, Joy Rohde, Ronald Grigor Suny

My dissertation, “The Space Age: Technological Prophecy and the Political Economy of Big Tech,” offers a novel social history of the emergence of the United States as a global space power from the origins of modern rocketry to the end of the Apollo program in 1972.
